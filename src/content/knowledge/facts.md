# Terry Mathew — Canonical Facts

Terry Mathew is a data product, analytics, and applied AI professional based in Bengaluru, India.

He is currently employed at Oracle as Senior Data Product Manager, Partner Analytics, a role he has held since March 2024.

Terry has worked at Oracle since May 2018, a little over eight years. During that time he moved from business operations and commercial transaction work into team leadership, analytics, and data product management.

His planned final working day at Oracle is 14 October 2026. That is a scheduled departure that has not yet happened, so until that date Oracle is his current employer.

His career has also included hospitality, retail, enterprise technical support, and HR shared services.

Terry's professional experience sits mainly at the intersection of product management, data and analytics, commercial systems, revenue operations, and applied AI.

He has worked with Finance, Sales, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Legal, Engineering, Deal Management, and partner-program stakeholders.

His professional experience includes product requirements, functional specifications, backlog prioritisation, user acceptance testing, KPI and metric definition, SQL, analytics, reporting, data reconciliation, commercial processes, and cross-functional delivery.

Terry is currently exploring roles and independent work involving data products, analytics, applied AI, and business systems. He is particularly interested in work where the problem is broader than producing dashboards and where he can own the definition of the problem, the product or workflow, and the outcome.

## Education

Terry completed a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Administration at the Indo-German Chamber of Commerce in Bangalore between 2011 and 2013.

He completed a B.Sc. in Hospitality and Hotel Administration at the Institute of Hotel Management in Trivandrum between 2006 and 2009.
