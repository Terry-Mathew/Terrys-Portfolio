# Terry Mathew — Professional Experience

Terry's experience at Oracle spans partner transactions, commercial operations, team leadership, partner analytics, and data product management. Across these roles, he worked on the operational processes behind partner programs and on the reporting and systems used to manage them.

## Senior Data Product Manager, Partner Analytics

Terry worked with Finance, Engineering, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, and partner-program stakeholders to define requirements and delivery priorities for analytics and operational-data workstreams. His work included assessment reporting, partner-credit system requirements, incentive eligibility analysis, data-quality controls, and reporting logic.

## Insights Analyst II, Partner Insights and Revenue Operations

Terry defined metrics with stakeholders and data engineers, built and maintained partner reporting, investigated changes in operational data, and used analysis to support leadership reviews and workload planning. He also redesigned recurring development and test reporting, using AI-assisted tooling to reduce repetitive preparation while retaining analyst validation.

## Business Operations Team Lead

Terry led an EMEA revenue-operations team. He coordinated workload, quality checks, service levels, coaching, and complex transaction escalations. He also improved deal-registration handling and documented procedures for unusual commercial workflows.

## Business Operations Specialist and Business Operations Analyst

Terry supported partner transactions and cloud sales processes. His work included deal registration, quote preparation, pricing and eligibility checks, approvals, contract reviews, exception handling, and booking handoffs.

## Earlier Experience

Before Oracle, Terry worked in HR shared services, enterprise technical support, retail operations, and hospitality. His earlier roles included process improvement, technical support, store operations, and food and beverage service.
