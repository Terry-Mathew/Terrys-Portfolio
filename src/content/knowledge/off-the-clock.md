# Terry Mathew — Outside Work

Outside work, Terry spends a lot of time around motorcycles, travel, photography, and learning things that have little to do with his job.

## Motorcycles

Terry rides a Bajaj Dominar and previously owned a Royal Enfield.

He prefers longer road trips to short rides around the city.

He enjoys riding with other people when a good route comes up, but is equally interested in the road trip itself rather than treating the motorcycle only as transport.

Some of his travel photography comes from these rides.

## Travel

Terry likes travelling to places that feel different from where he normally lives.

Recent international travel has included Indonesia and Malaysia, with stops including Yogyakarta, the Bromo region, Bali, Langkawi, and Kuala Lumpur.

He is interested in slower travel rather than only visiting the major tourist attractions.

Road trips are another part of that interest, particularly routes through coastal areas and places where the journey itself is part of the experience.

## Photography

Terry takes photographs while travelling rather than treating photography as a separate activity.

His photographs tend to include motorcycles, roads, landscapes, old buildings and forts, streets, sunsets, and birds.

Several of the photographs used on his portfolio site come from his own travels.

## Birds

Terry likes birds and tends to notice and photograph them when travelling or spending time outdoors.

This is a genuine interest rather than a professional project.

## Things he wants to learn or try

Terry is interested in experiences that involve learning a physical skill or doing something outside his normal routine.

These include scuba diving, surfing, Muay Thai, skydiving, and similar adventure activities.

These should be described as interests or things he wants to try unless he has explicitly completed them.

## Creating things outside his job

Terry also spends time building independent software and AI projects.

More recently, he has been experimenting with writing and video content around work, careers, uncertainty, technology, and some of the ideas he finds interesting.

He has also been learning to cook more regularly.

The personal side of the portfolio should not try to turn every hobby into a professional lesson. These are simply things Terry enjoys or is curious about.
